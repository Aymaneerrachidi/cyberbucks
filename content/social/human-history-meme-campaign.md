# CyberBucks: digital currency history comes alive

## Campaign spine

**Before Bitcoin, there was CyberBucks. Now the story comes alive on Robinhood Chain.**

CyberBucks has a stronger position than a generic new memecoin: the name has documented digital-currency history. DigiCash opened its public eCash trial in 1994 using CyberBucks as the trial currency. The current CyberBucks community project is a new, independent project planned for Robinhood Chain. It carries the name and story forward; it is not the original 1994 financial instrument.

The five artworks are text-free, realistic editorial memes at 1080 x 1080. Put the hook, history, and disclaimer in the post caption.

## Recommended five-post sequence

1. Post **Always has been** first. It states the campaign idea in the most familiar format.
2. Post **Distracted by the OG** next. It connects the history to Robinhood Chain's meme culture.
3. Use **Woman versus history cat** to open a factual lore thread.
4. Post **Vlad finds the original** with the independence disclaimer in the same post.
5. Finish with **Van Gogh** as a culture-first evergreen post that does not depend on chain metrics.

## Posting guardrails

- Use "planned for Robinhood Chain" until deployment is complete and independently verifiable.
- Never imply that Robinhood, Vlad Tenev, DigiCash, or David Chaum sponsors or endorses the current project.
- Do not describe the current project as the same financial instrument issued during the 1994 DigiCash trial.
- Say the project carries the name, history, or story forward.
- Avoid price targets, profit promises, fake scarcity, and invented contract addresses.
- Keep market-share and volume figures dated because they change quickly.
